# GEO105 — Outside CRS area of use

**Scope:** dataset  
**Default severity:** warning  
**Certainty:** deterministic

Checks projected datasets against the area of use published by the CRS
definition. Dataset bounds are transformed to geographic coordinates before
comparison.

**PASS:** the observed extent overlaps the CRS area of use.  
**FAIL:** the observed extent is outside the CRS area of use.  
**UNKNOWN:** CRS, area-of-use, or transform evidence is unavailable.  
**NOT_APPLICABLE:** the CRS is not projected.

A failure can indicate either an inappropriate projected CRS or incorrect CRS
metadata. GeoDebug does not choose a replacement CRS automatically.
