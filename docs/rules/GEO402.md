# GEO402 — No spatial overlap

**Scope:** relation  
**Default severity:** error  
**Certainty:** deterministic

Compares two dataset extents in a common CRS. A failure means their transformed
bounding boxes do not overlap.

**PASS:** transformed extents overlap.  
**FAIL:** transformed extents do not overlap.  
**UNKNOWN:** comparable bounds cannot be established.  
**NOT_APPLICABLE:** the context does not contain exactly two subjects.

Bounding-box overlap is not treated as proof that geometries intersect.
